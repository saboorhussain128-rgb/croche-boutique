Cherish Your Knits

Yarn Care & Longevity Guide

Every crochet creation is made with mindful stitches. With proper gentle care, your handmade pieces will remain as soft, colorful, and perfectly shaped as day one.

Wearables & Tops

For shrugs, crop tops, sweaters, and dresses crafted with milk cotton: Hand wash in cold water using a drop of gentle wool detergent. Gently press out excess water—never wring or twist.

Velvet & Plushies

For velvet chenille amigurumi and pouches: Spot clean outer fabric with a damp microfiber cloth and mild soap. Avoid submerging in water to protect inner polyfill fluffiness.

Baby Rompers & Caps

Woven with ultra-gentle, hypoallergenic milk cotton: Hand wash with baby-safe detergent. Smooth flat to reshape the cap brim and dry away from direct heat or harsh sun.

Table Runners & Decor

For macrame lace runners and coasters: Soak in lukewarm water with mild soap. Lay flat on blocking mats or a towel and gently pin/steam block on lowest setting with a pressing cloth.

Artisan Best Practices (Do's)

  • Hand wash in cold or lukewarm water (under 30°C / 86°F).
  • Roll in a clean, dry towel like a sushi roll to absorb moisture.
  • Reshape garments while damp and dry flat in shaded breeze.
  • Fold wearables loosely—never hang on wire hangers.

Pitfalls to Avoid (Don'ts)

  • Never twist, wring, or pull wet crochet fibers.
  • Do not use bleach, optical brighteners, or harsh enzymes.
  • Avoid high-heat tumble dryers (causes fiber shrinkage).
  • Do not iron directly on acrylic or chenille without pressing cloth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering handcrafted crochet.

How long does a custom crochet order take?
Since every piece is stitched individually by hand, custom orders typically take 5 to 10 business days to craft depending on size and detail. For rush gifts or specific event deadlines, let us know in your inquiry and we'll do our best to accommodate!
Can I request colors or designs not shown on Instagram?
Yes, absolutely! We love custom requests. You can describe your dream color palette or send us reference pictures via the inquiry form or directly through WhatsApp.
How do I care for and wash my crochet pieces?
Handmade crochet is gentle. We recommend hand washing in cold water with a mild detergent, gently pressing out excess water (never wring or twist), and reshaping it flat on a clean towel to dry.
How does payment and shipping work?
Once you submit an inquiry, we verify details with you and provide payment instructions (bank transfer, online payments, or digital wallets depending on your region). We package each piece securely with tracking provided upon dispatch.
