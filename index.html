Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering handcrafted crochet.

How long does a custom crochet order take?
Since every piece is stitched individually by hand, custom orders typically take 5 to 10 business days to craft depending on size and detail. For rush gifts or specific event deadlines, let us know in your inquiry and we'll do our best to accommodate!
Can I request colors or designs not shown on Instagram?
Yes, absolutely! We love custom requests. You can describe your dream color palette or send us reference pictures via the inquiry form or directly through WhatsApp.
How do I care for and wash my crochet pieces?
Handmade crochet is gentle. We recommend hand washing in cold water with a mild detergent, gently pressing out excess water (never wring or twist), and reshaping it flat on a clean towel to dry.
How does payment and shipping work?
Once you submit an inquiry, we verify details with you and provide payment instructions (bank transfer, online payments, or digital wallets depending on your region). We package each piece securely with tracking provided upon dispatch.
